Privacy Policy

At HairGlow, we prioritize your privacy and data security. Learn how we collect, protect, and handle your information.

Last Updated: January 2026

1 Information We Collect

When you order products from HairGlow or create an account, we collect information required to complete your transaction and provide superior customer service:

  • Personal Details: Full Name, Email Address, and Phone Number.
  • Shipping Information: Street Address, City, Postal Code, and Delivery Instructions.
  • Payment Information: Payment method preference (Cash on Delivery, Easypaisa, JazzCash, or Bank Transfer).

2 How We Use Your Information

Your personal information is strictly used for legitimate business purposes:

  • Processing, confirming, and dispatching your hair growth oil orders across Pakistan.
  • Sending SMS or WhatsApp updates regarding parcel tracking and order confirmation.
  • Providing prompt customer support and addressing product inquiries or refunds.
  • Improving website functionality and user experience.

3 Data Security & Protection

We implement industry-standard 256-bit SSL encryption to protect data transmitted to and from our website. Your confidential credentials and passwords are hashed using secure cryptographic algorithms. We do NOT store credit card PINs or bank login details on our servers.

4 Third-Party Sharing

HairGlow respects your privacy. We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third-party marketers. We only share essential details (Name, Address, Phone) with trusted courier logistics partners (e.g. TCS, Leopard, CallCourier) solely for parcel delivery purposes.

5 Your Rights & Control

You have full control over your data. You may log into your User Dashboard at any time to review your profile, order history, or request full account deletion by contacting our support team at support@hairglow.com.

Have Questions About Our Privacy Policy?

If you have any concerns regarding data privacy, feel free to contact our customer care team.
