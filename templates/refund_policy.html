Refund & Exchange Policy

We stand behind the premium quality of HairGlow. Enjoy a hassle-free 7-day return and replacement guarantee.

Effective Date: January 2026

1 7-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We offer a 7-Day Return and Exchange Guarantee from the date of parcel delivery. If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect product, you are entitled to a full replacement or refund.

2 Eligibility Criteria for Returns

To be eligible for an exchange or refund, the item must meet the following criteria:

  • The return request must be submitted within 7 days of delivery.
  • The product must be in its original packaging along with proof of purchase / order receipt.
  • For damaged items, please share a clear unboxing photo or video via WhatsApp/Email.

3 How to Request a Return or Exchange

Step 1: Contact our support team via WhatsApp or Email at support@hairglow.com.

Step 2: Provide your Order Number (e.g. #HG-1024) and describe the reason for return/exchange.

Step 3: Once approved, dispatch the parcel back to our fulfillment hub. A fresh replacement bottle or refund will be processed immediately upon receipt.

4 Refund Method & Timelines

Approved refunds will be processed back to your preferred payment account (Easypaisa, JazzCash, or Bank Account) within 3 to 5 business days after item inspection.

Need Immediate Help With a Return?

Reach out to our customer care team right away to initiate your return or replacement.
